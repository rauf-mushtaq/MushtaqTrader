# MushtaqTrader
Practice trading with fake money yet still be able to learn how to trade without losing money

## MushtaqTrack (`racer/`)

A low-poly time-trial racer in the style of PolyTrack, in a single HTML file (`racer/index.html`, with Three.js and Ammo.js loaded from CDNs). Open the file in a browser to play.

- **Car physics**: runs on [Ammo.js](https://github.com/kripken/ammo.js), the JavaScript port of the Bullet physics engine. Each car is a Bullet raycast vehicle: a rigid chassis on four raycast wheels with sprung suspension. Grip comes from a friction-circle tyre model that uses each wheel's load from Bullet. The road is Bullet collision geometry built from the track; barriers, pillars and blocks are handled by the car itself, so brushing one slides you along it instead of spinning you round. It's very grippy, with top speeds kept moderate so you stay in control through the turns (the speedometer reads 70% of the true speed, about 115–140 km/h flat out), and you get weight transfer, wheelspin and real slides. The road is a true 3D surface, so cars can drive banked turns, ride walls and go upside down through loops. A small handling layer on top adds the drift, boosts and air control, and keeps the car on banked turns, wall rides and loops. As in PolyTrack, a car going fast enough flies off the top of a ramp or a crest. After a landing the throttle cuts briefly so the back wheels can settle.
- **Air control**: in the air, steering turns the car and Space works as an air brake that stops it spinning. The car lines itself up with the road it's coming down on, so it lands flat on its wheels, and a very big drop is cushioned.
- **Drift**: the cars grip and stay straight through corners. To drift, hold Space (the handbrake) as you turn in to slide the tail out, keep steering into the slide to hold it, then straighten up for a small drift boost; longer drifts give bigger boosts.
- **Track pieces**: road, tight, wide and **banked** turns, **wall rides**, **loops** and **massive loops** (35 m tall; both exit a lane left or right, and the car turns with the road through them unless you steer), **plazas** (open squares with no barriers; raised up, a plaza is the roof of a building with solid walls), ramps, **long slopes** and **steep ramps**, kickers, boost pads, **pillars** and **chicane blocks**, **tunnels**, checkpoints, and arrow signs on tight turns.
- **Environments**: Summer, Winter (snowy pines) and Desert (cacti and mesas), combined with time of day.
- **14 cars** in the garage, each with its own model and handling: an open-wheel formula car, an Italian-style supercar, a rear-engine German-style GT, a Le Mans-style prototype, a hypercar, an electric hypercar, a stock car, a muscle car, a drift coupé, a rally hatch, an off-road pickup, a classic roadster and a go-kart. You can repaint any of them. The names are made up and there are no real brand logos.
- **83 official tracks** across Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert, **Sky**, **Stunt** and **Circuit**, plus a **daily track** and **endless random tracks** from a seed you can share. The generator uses every piece type.
- **Medals** (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Author), and a **ghost of your best run** with live split deltas.
- **Sky tracks**: rows of boost pads, a climb up steep ramps, and a big jump onto a platform in the air with the finish on it.
- **Stunt tracks**: 25 tracks packed with loops, massive loops, kicker jumps, steep-ramp launches, wall rides, banked turns, tunnels and chicanes, from Loop Street up to Grand Finale.
- **Circuit tracks**: 20 closed laps in the style of Grand Prix circuits, with the finish line just behind the start: long straights with boost pads, heavy-braking hairpins, chicanes, esses, fast sweepers, banked corners, hills, a figure-eight with a bridge and a banked oval. The names are made up.
- **Steep ramps** climb two levels in one piece and shape themselves to what joins them: from flat road one curves up smoothly, steep ramps in a row make one long climb, and a steep ramp with nothing after it launches you into the air.
- **Track editor** with 21 piece types, height levels, an environment picker, undo, a route check, and `MT1.` share codes. On a tablet or phone, tap to place a piece, drag with one finger to move around, and pinch with two fingers to zoom.
- **Your name**: the first time you open the game it asks for your name or nickname (on the hosted page it starts from the name on your account). Other players see it on leaderboards, community tracks, ghosts, live races and chat. Change it any time with Change on the menu or in Settings.
- **Community tracks** (on the hosted page): press Publish on a track under Yours and everyone who plays the game sees it in the Community tab, with its maker's name. They can race it or copy it into their own editor. You can update or unpublish your own tracks, and the game's owner can remove any track. Publishing needs Contributor access or higher to the page; people with view-only access can still race community tracks. A copy of the game opened as a local file has no Community tab.
- **Leaderboards** (on the hosted page): every player's best time on each track goes on a shared board, showing their name and car. Each track card shows the record and your place, and you can click it to see the full board. The finish screen shows the top eight and where you placed, and the HUD shows the record while you race. A board belongs to the track's layout, so the daily, a random seed, a community track or an imported code all have boards too. Times from editor test drives aren't posted. Posting needs Contributor access or higher; view-only players can still see the boards. The game's owner can remove a time. A local copy shows only your own best runs.
- **Live races** (on the hosted page): press Race online to host a race or join one from the list of open races; while someone is hosting, everyone else on the menu gets an invitation with a Join button. The host picks the track (any official track, the daily, a random track or a community track) and presses Start. Everyone gets the same 3-2-1 countdown and races at the same time. Other racers show as see-through cars with their names, on the track and the minimap, and the HUD shows live positions by checkpoints. The results list each racer's finish time; places go by time, and each player's clock runs from their own "Go". In a live race R takes you back to your last checkpoint instead of restarting the clock. If the host leaves, another player takes over hosting. The room has a chat: someone who joins sees the last couple of minutes of messages, and the finish screen tells you when new messages are waiting. Nothing about a live race or its chat is stored; it works for anyone who can open the page, including view-only players.
- **The City** (on the hosted page): under Race online, a free-roam practice map that anyone can drive into at any time and drive around with everyone else who's there. The whole floor is open, with a Rooftop Run (jump from roof to roof across the alleys), a skate park with funboxes, kickers, a loop and a steep launch, a stunt yard with a massive loop and a ramp pyramid, and Big Air, which launches you onto a roof 20 m up. There's no clock: R takes you back to the start and Enter puts your car back on its wheels.
- **Ghosts of the top times**: a time that makes a track's top ten is posted with its ghost. On a leaderboard (or the finish screen), switch on Ghost next to other players' times to race against them, up to five at once. Each ghost is a see-through copy of that player's car with their name above it, and it shows on the minimap too. The first time you open a board, the record's ghost is switched on for you.
- Red/white barriers on every edge (you can clear them when airborne), skid marks, tyre smoke, engine sound, three cameras (the chase camera rolls with the car through loops), a minimap, and keyboard, gamepad and large touch controls. On tablets and phones the 3D view is drawn at a lower resolution (with smaller shadows) so it runs smoothly; the menus and text stay sharp.
- An autopilot drives the menu background. It was used to test all 14 cars on all 83 official tracks, and to set the medal times.

Your times, ghosts, custom tracks and settings are saved in the browser's local storage.

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W / ↑ | Accelerate (the clock starts on your first input) |
| S / ↓ | Brake, then reverse |
| A D / ← → | Steer (also in the air) |
| Space / Shift | Handbrake; air brake in the air |
| R | Instant restart |
| Enter | Back to last checkpoint |
| C | Switch camera |
| Esc / P | Pause |
| M | Mute |
| G | Garage (from the menu) |
